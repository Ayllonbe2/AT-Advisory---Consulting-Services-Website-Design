What I do

Four things worth paying an outsider for

01

AI portfolio review & rationalisation

A structured read of every AI and data initiative you fund: what deserves more, what belongs with a better-fit owner, and what should be closed. I have made all three calls on platforms I owned myself and defended them in front of senior leadership.

02

MLOps platform setup & reusable templates

The plumbing that makes the second project cheaper than the first: standardised pipelines, a peer-reviewed template library, and adoption measured through active users and time saved per user rather than slideware.

03

Build-vs-buy & vendor evaluation

Proof-of-concept design, benchmarking of competing tools and a written recommendation you can hand to a budget holder — covering delivery and partnership models, not only licence prices.

04

Team building, hiring & mentoring

Hiring plans, interview design, career paths and coaching for the managers who report to you — including the underperformance conversations most organisations route around.

How we work
One day

Advisory day / diagnostic

A single day, remote or on site, on one decision you have to make. You leave with the recommendation in writing.

Weeks

Fixed-scope project

A defined deliverable within a few weeks: a portfolio review, a vendor benchmark, a platform blueprint or a hiring plan.

Days per month

Fractional AI leadership

Recurring presence as the AI leader you do not yet have — typically two to four days a month, steering the roadmap and the people.

Capacity is deliberately limited to two to four days a month alongside a full-time platform role, so engagements are scoped around decisions rather than staffing. Working languages: Spanish, English and French. Based in Madrid, remote across Europe.

How I decide

Build the bench, not just the backlog

Deliberate hiring, individual career paths and coaching of the managers who report into the role — including the hard conversations, because a team's ceiling is set by its weakest link.

Be willing to kill your own product

I recommended closing a platform I owned once the ROI and adoption data no longer supported it, and transferred another to a better-fit owner — both defended with numbers in front of senior leadership.

Translate between worlds

Challenging model choices with ML engineers in the morning and framing the same problem as a business case for executives in the afternoon. This is where most analytics value is won or lost.
