My approach

Three possible paths for every product

This is not about judging anyone's work, but about understanding where each product stands today. Using the same indicators for all of them, we build together a recommendation the team can understand, share and carry out.

Path 01

Grow

The product is delivering value and has room to grow. We give it focus, resources and goals so it keeps moving forward.

  • More and more people use it
  • Its return is clear and measurable
  • It fits where the company is heading
Path 02

Make it sustainable

The product is valuable, but today it costs more than it should. Together we find ways to lighten it — operating costs, vendors, processes or scope — without losing what makes it useful.

  • High cost for the value it delivers
  • Vendor or operating costs above what is needed
  • Overlaps with other products in the portfolio
Path 03

Close with care

Sometimes a product has already done its job. When that happens, we plan a respectful transition: supporting customers, recognising the team's work and protecting what has been learned, so the change frees up energy instead of creating uncertainty.

  • Declining sales or usage
  • No realistic prospect of return
  • A better alternative already exists for customers
What we look at

Sales, margin and profitability · customers and active usage · retention and satisfaction · cost per customer and per unit · total cost of ownership (TCO) · ROI and payback · operational burden · vendor dependency · strategic fit.

How I can help

An outside view — close, honest and grounded in data

01

Product portfolio review

Together we go through every product, service or business line you fund, listening to the people who use them and make them possible. The result is a shared view and a prioritised plan to bring to leadership.

02

KPI analysis & product scorecard

We define the indicators that truly reflect each product's value, making the most of the data you already have, and build a simple scorecard to follow its progress quarter by quarter.

03

Cost & vendor optimisation

We look at the full cost of each product and find sensible savings: vendors, processes, operations or overlapping products — always with the estimated saving on the table before deciding.

04

Thoughtful transitions & closures

Closing a product is also part of its story. I plan transitions that look after customers, give the team a meaningful next step, preserve what has been learned and communicate the change openly.

05

New initiatives & business cases

Before launching, buying or scaling something new, we look at it together: the problem it solves, a realistic business case and the indicators that will tell us, early on, whether it is working.

06

Supporting teams through change

Decisions only work if people embrace them. I help leaders communicate them, reorganise teams with sense and adopt a way of managing by indicators that stays after I leave.
