Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Data controller

Aarón Ayllón Benítez, a self-employed professional trading as AT Advisory (Ayllon Tech Advisory), based in Madrid (Spain). For anything related to your data, write to me at ayllonbenitez.aaron@gmail.com.

2. What data I process

Only what you give me when you contact me, through the contact form or by email: your name, your email address, the name of your company or business (if you include it) and the content of your message. Please do not include particularly sensitive data, such as health information.

3. What I use it for

To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask me to, prepare a proposal. I do not use it to send you advertising or newsletters, and I make no automated decisions with it.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, which you give when you send the enquiry (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). If the enquiry concerns a possible engagement, also taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before.

5. How long I keep it

For as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and then for a maximum of 12 months in case we pick up the conversation again, unless you ask me to delete it sooner. If we end up working together, I will keep only what is necessary for the periods required by law (for example, tax rules).

6. Who else receives it

I do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. To run this website and my email, I rely on these providers, which can access your data only to provide their service:

  • Google (Gmail), where I receive and store email.
  • Web3Forms, the service that delivers the contact form messages to my inbox. It does not store them (it only forwards them) and its servers are in the United States.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts this website and, like any web server, logs technical data about visits, such as the IP address.

Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, its rectification or erasure and the restriction of its processing, object to it or request its portability by writing to ayllonbenitez.aaron@gmail.com. I will reply within one month at the latest. If you think I have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Cookies and third-party resources

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or tracking tools, and it does not load third-party resources: fonts, images and scripts are served from the website itself.

9. Changes to this policy

If I change it, I will publish the new version here with its date.
