My approach

Three steps to modernise your business in the right order

Technology works when it comes in the right order. First we understand your business and lay solid foundations; then we add AI where it brings real value; and finally we make sure both your customers and AI assistants can find you.

Step 01

Lay the foundations

We digitalise what is holding you back today: a clear website, customer and sales data in order, and simple tools for your team's day-to-day work. Affordable, right-sized and left in your hands.

  • Information scattered across emails, paper and spreadsheets
  • Repetitive manual tasks that eat up hours every week
  • A website that is outdated, slow or hard to update
Step 02

Add AI where it pays off

The AI that pays off most in a small business doesn't talk to your customers: it works behind the scenes. It reads invoices and orders, prepares drafts your team reviews and anticipates sales and stock. We only bring it in when it saves time or money, and in a way your team can manage without depending on anyone.

  • Data typed by hand from one document to another
  • Hours spent writing quotes, emails or reports
  • Purchasing and stock decided by gut feeling
Step 03

Be found by AI

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity before searching on Google. We make your business information clear, consistent and easy for these systems to interpret, so they have good reasons to recommend you. No one can guarantee a recommendation, but a great deal can be done to earn one.

  • AI assistants don't mention you, or get your details wrong
  • Incomplete or outdated information on Google and directories
  • A website that doesn't answer what your customers ask
What we look at

How customers and AI assistants find you today · website and Google Business Profile · manual and repetitive processes · state of your data · the tools you pay for and the ones you actually use · reviews and reputation · data protection and security · budget and team capacity · growth goals.
